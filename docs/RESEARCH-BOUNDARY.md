# Research boundary

This repository is a first paper draft and measurement program.

## What is asserted

- Causal compression is defined as contract-relative reduction of future decision burden while preserving continuation-relevant distinctions.
- Adaptive systems can be represented as evolving attributed transition networks with queues, maintained stocks, and retirement flows.
- Raw capability, adaptive capacity, maintenance burden, and free headroom are distinct variables.
- The framework produces bounded predictions about queues, maintenance, retirement, and state compression.

## What is not asserted

- No universal law about civilization, organizations, brains, or markets.
- No claim that opacity establishes causality or reliability.
- No P/NP result.
- No energy result.
- No empirical estimate of AI's social or economic effects.
- No claim that the scalar headroom model captures nonlinear networks without extension.

## Evidence labels

`theory`: formal definition or proposed mechanism.

`bounded_hypothesis`: testable claim requiring a named future instrument.

`source_grounded_context`: claim supported by a cited external source.

`not_empirical`: no real-world dataset or field observation is included in this repository.
