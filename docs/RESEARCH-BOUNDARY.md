# Research boundary

This repository is a conceptual theory paper and measurement program. The paper's central mechanism is causal compression: historically expensive causal sequences become maintained transitions that are cheap to invoke under a declared continuation contract.

## Central architecture

```text
causal compression
-> cheap invocation
-> expanded reachability
-> selection
-> commitment
-> maintenance burden
-> headroom loss
-> renewal, replacement, or retirement
```

The paper distinguishes two coupled but non-identical forms:

- representational causal compression: history is reduced to a future-sufficient present state;
- structural causal compression: historical work is embodied in maintained infrastructure that makes a transition cheaply available.

## What is asserted as framework

- Causal compression is a contract-relative mechanism, not a synonym for causal understanding.
- The invocation-construction gap is a proposed observable asymmetry between establishing reliable capability and invoking it locally.
- Compressed transitions can expand reachable states while creating commitments that require maintenance.
- Raw capability, adaptive capacity, commitment burden, and adaptive headroom are distinct constructs.
- Safety of retirement and positive headroom release are distinct predicates.
- The lifecycle of compressed capability includes generation, evaluation, selection, compilation, maintenance, re-evaluation, and renewal, replacement, or retirement.

## What remains hypothesis

- Larger invocation-construction gaps are associated with stronger reports of magical appearance.
- Increasing candidate generation moves bottlenecks downstream when evaluation, integration, maintenance, or retirement capacity does not scale.
- Commitment burden and maintenance drift can narrow effective reachable futures while nominal capabilities grow.
- An intermediate structural-update regime can outperform both excessively rapid and excessively slow updating under environmental change.
- Systems with larger compression gaps are more sensitive to maintenance or provenance failures unless substitutes exist.

## What is not asserted

- No universal law about civilization, organizations, brains, markets, or artificial intelligence.
- No claim that representational and structural compression are identical.
- No claim that scalar headroom captures nonlinear networks without extension.
- No claim that positive headroom release is required for every safe retirement.
- No claim that opacity establishes causality or reliability.
- No empirical estimate of AI's social or economic effects.
- No energy result.
- No P/NP result.

## Evidence labels

`framework`: conceptual mechanism or model architecture.

`definition`: vocabulary or explicitly typed model object.

`bounded_hypothesis`: testable claim requiring a named future instrument.

`source_grounded_context`: claim supported by a cited external source.

`not_empirical`: no real-world dataset or field observation is included in this repository.

Synthetic fixtures may validate an instrument or implementation invariant, but cannot establish the broad framework without external observation and matched controls.
